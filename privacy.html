Privacy policy

fitair is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed: the owner's own data from the Google Health API, using read-only scopes only.
  • Use: the data is used solely to display charts and generate personal insights for the owner.
  • Storage: data is stored privately on infrastructure controlled by the owner.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
